Snake plant

  • It need bright and indirect light
  • Avoid harsh direct sunlight
  • Keep warm and protect from cold drafts
  • Water deeply every 2 to 3 weeks in summer
  • Choose a pot with a drainagehold to prevent standing

English ivy

  • Provide bright and indirect light
  • Allow the top inch of soil to dry out before watering deeply
  • Prefer high humidity
  • Feed with a balanced , dilute liquid fertilizer evry two to four weeks during summer and spring
